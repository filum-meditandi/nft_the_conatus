### Pain Scale Reference
```
0     = No pain
1-3   = Mild pain (noticeable but doesn't interfere with activities)
4-6   = Moderate pain (interferes with tasks, requires adjustment)
7-9   = Severe pain (significantly limits function, hard to concentrate)
10    = Worst possible pain (completely debilitating)
```
