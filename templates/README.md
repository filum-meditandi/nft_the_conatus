## Overview

These templates are designed for **injured plaintiffs** to systematically document their pain, suffering, medical treatment, and expenses. They support the Phenomenological Evidence System by providing structured formats for:

- **Daily logging** of pain levels, symptoms, and expenses
- **Weekly summaries** to consolidate daily records
- **Monthly reviews** for pattern analysis and totals
- **Yearly summaries** for comprehensive case documentation

## Template Hierarchy

```
DAILY LOGS (7/week)
    ↓ consolidate into
WEEKLY SUMMARIES (4-5/month)
    ↓ consolidate into
MONTHLY REVIEWS (12/year)
    ↓ consolidate into
YEARLY SUMMARY (1/year)
```

## Design Principles

### Accessibility-First
These templates follow the system's constitutional accessibility constraints:

- **Simple, clear language** - No medical jargon required
- **Checkbox options** - Minimize writing when brain fog is present
- **Structured sections** - Easy to navigate and complete partially
- **Large write-in areas** - Space for handwritten notes
- **Consistent formatting** - Predictable layout across all templates

### Legal Documentation Standards
Templates are designed to produce legally defensible evidence:

- **Dated entries** - Clear timestamps for all records
- **Signature lines** - Authentication of entries
- **Receipt tracking** - Document expense verification
- **Narrative sections** - Capture subjective experience in own words
- **Running totals** - Easy calculation of cumulative damages

### SMS Integration
Each template includes SMS submission shortcuts compatible with the Phenomenological Evidence System:

```
Daily:   Text "LOG" followed by key data
Weekly:  Text "WEEK [#] PAIN [avg] COST [total]"
Monthly: Text "MONTH [#] PAIN [avg] TOTAL $[amount]"
Yearly:  Text "YEAR [#] PAIN [avg] TOTAL $[amount]"
```

## How to Use

### For Clients

1. **Print Templates**
   - Print sufficient copies for your tracking period
   - Daily logs: 7 per week
   - Weekly summaries: 4-5 per month
   - Monthly reviews: 1 per month
   - Yearly summary: 1 per year

2. **Complete Daily**
   - Fill out the Daily Log each day
   - Morning, midday, and evening sections
   - Don't skip days - even "no change" is valuable data

3. **Weekly Review**
   - Every weekend, complete the Weekly Summary
   - Transfer totals from your 7 daily logs
   - Attach daily logs behind the weekly summary

4. **Monthly Consolidation**
   - At month end, complete the Monthly Review
   - Calculate totals from weekly summaries
   - Attach all weekly summaries and daily logs

5. **Annual Summary**
   - At year end, complete the Yearly Summary
   - This is your comprehensive annual case document
   - Review with your attorney

### For Legal Teams

These templates provide:
- **Consistent documentation** across all clients
- **Quantified data** for damage calculations
- **Narrative evidence** of pain and suffering
- **Expense tracking** with receipt references
- **Trend analysis** data for expert testimony

- Activity limitations
- Quality of life changes

## Printing Recommendations

For best results when printing these templates:

- **Paper size:** 8.5" x 11" (US Letter)
- **Orientation:** Portrait
- **Margins:** Standard (1 inch)
- **Font rendering:** Enable markdown rendering or print as-is
- **Consider:** Using a binder with dividers for organization

## Digital Submission

Templates can also be submitted digitally:
1. Complete the template in a text editor or markdown app
2. Submit via the system's SMS gateway
3. Upload scanned handwritten forms
4. Use the API endpoint for structured submission
